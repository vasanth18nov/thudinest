How to play
  • Three shots. Clear sends it high and deep (a lift from low down). Drop plays it short (a net shot near the net). Smash hits down hard from overhead (a drive or push from lower).
  • Press the shot just before the shuttle reaches you; the racket swings when it arrives. The ring under your feet (and the buttons, on a phone) light up lime while a press would be Perfect.
  • Jump smash: press Smash early, as the ring turns lime, on a high shuttle, and you leap to hit it at the top of the jump. It's the fastest shot in the game, but it's riskier and you land a moment later. Press too late and you smash from the ground.
  • With auto-run on, your player runs to the shuttle and the stick (or WASD) aims: left and right across the court, up for deeper, down for shorter. With it off, the stick moves you and aims as you hit.
  • Keys: J clear, K drop, L smash, C switches the camera, P or Esc pauses. A game controller works too: A clear, B drop, X or RT smash, Y camera.
  • Rally scoring: every rally wins a point. Serve from the right when your score is even, the left when it's odd.
