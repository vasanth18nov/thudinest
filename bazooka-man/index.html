How to Play

  • Aim: click/tap and drag away from the bazooka, like a slingshot pulled backward.
  • Power: the further you drag, the harder the shot — watch the power bar.
  • Fire: let go to launch. Rockets fall under gravity and explode on impact.
  • Keyboard: Up/Down aim the angle, Left/Right switch rocket type, hold Space to charge power and release to fire.
  • Barrels explode everything nearby — chain a few together for big blasts.
  • Bounce rockets skip off the ground and platforms before exploding — good for tricky angles.
  • Break down the building — the blast usually gets whoever's behind it too.
  • Clear the level with as few rockets as possible for 3 stars.
