Mic processing
Delay check
Why can there be delay? This app adds almost nothing. It sends your mic straight to the speaker with no extra buffering. The rest comes from your hardware. Bluetooth speakers, headphones and mics each add about 150–250 ms that no website or app can remove. For instant sound use the built-in mic or a wired/USB mic, plus wired headphones or speakers.

🎤 How to sing

  1. Tap the mic button and allow microphone access. You'll hear yourself straight away.
  2. Pick a voice effect. Echo, Hall and Stadium add the big karaoke sound.
  3. Add a song from your device, or paste a YouTube karaoke link. Synced lyrics load automatically.
  4. Press play and sing. Use Record to save your performance.

Squealing? That's the speaker feeding back into the mic. Turn the mic volume down, point the mic away from the speaker, or wear headphones.

Hearing a delay? That's almost always Bluetooth. Use the built-in mic or a wired mic and speakers for instant sound. Setup → Measure shows the real number.
