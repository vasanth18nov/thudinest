02 · How we work

One crew, start to handover

We stay with a site from the first visit to the final sign-off, so nothing gets lost between contractors.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Site visit, measurements and an agreed scope before any work starts.

  2. 02

    Groundwork

    Foundations, plinths, fencing and compound preparation.

  3. 03

    Erection

    Tower or pole sections lifted, bolted and plumbed true.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Antennas, RRUs, microwave, cabinets, power and lines.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Labelled, weatherproofed, photographed and signed off.

Quality

Done right, up there

A site is only as good as the work you can't see from the ground. These are the details we don't skip.

  • Every cable labelled

    Sector, band and port tagged at both ends, so the next engineer on site knows exactly what goes where.

  • Weatherproofed to last

    Connectors sealed against Malaysian rain, heat and humidity — the main reason outdoor connections fail.

  • Tidy, traceable routing

    Cables dressed, clamped and colour-banded, inside the cabinet and up the tower.

  • Photo-documented

    Each stage photographed with time and location for your records and acceptance.

04 · About us

A contractor you can call directly

Alisons Construction Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysian-registered telecommunications contractor based at Plaza Sg Besi in Kuala Lumpur. We take on site work for network operators, tower companies and main contractors — from building the structure to installing and cabling the equipment that goes on it.

Our crews work on lattice towers, monopoles, rooftop and water-tank sites, from Kuala Lumpur down to Muar, Johor. When you call, you speak to the person who runs the work.
