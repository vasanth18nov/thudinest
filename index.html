Where exactly is Thudinest?

On MTP Road in Poombuhar Nagar, Thudiyalur, Coimbatore. Open it in Google Maps for directions.

How do I book?

Book instantly on Airbnb at airbnb.com/h/thudinest, or WhatsApp us if you have questions first.

What's included in the stay?

A comfortable bedroom, attached bathroom, living area and basic kitchen amenities, plus free Wi-Fi, free parking, towels, soap and drinking water.

Who is it best for?

Couples, solo travellers and remote workers looking for something a little different. It's a tiny home: compact, but thoughtfully designed for comfort.

Are there any house rules?

Please respect quiet hours after 10 PM. Unmarried couples are not allowed to stay. The home is on the main road, so there may be some light traffic noise.

What is Thudinest website hosting?

If you have an access code from us, you can create a free single-page website for your business in about two minutes, with no coding, and update it any time. Get started.

Are the games really free?

Yes. All 19 games run in your browser with no download and no account, including 3D cricket (Floodlit Over) and Guest Rush.
