How to play
  • Two buttons. A is the main touch: the jump serve, the pass, the set, the spike and the block. B is the soft one: the float serve, a shot over the block, or attacking on two when you'd set.
  • Timing is everything: press just before the ball reaches you. The ring under your feet (and the buttons, on a phone) light up while a press would be Perfect. For a spike or a block the press is your jump, so take off as the ring lights.
  • Serving: A tosses for a jump serve, B for a float. Press again as the ball comes down to hit it. The stick aims: left and right, up for deeper.
  • Your partner plays the touches you don't: pass, you set and they spike, or the other way round. When the other side attacks, whoever is at the net blocks and the other digs.
  • With Run to the ball for me on, you get to the ball on your own and the stick aims your serve and your spike. Off, the stick moves you too.
  • Keys: J or Space for A, K for B, WASD or the arrows to move and aim, C camera, P pause, M sound. A game controller works too.
  • Real rules: three touches a side, a point on every rally, sets to 21 (the third to 15), won by two clear points. The lines are in, the antennae are out, and a ball off the tape plays on.
