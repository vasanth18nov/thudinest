GuestRush!

Sunset at the homestay and the guests keep coming. Carry the bags, find the charger, water the garden and welcome everyone before their patience runs out.

Enter · Space · or click

How to play

  1. Guests arrive at the gate one at a time with a request in a speech bubble.
  2. Follow the gold marker: fetch, carry, water or guide, then report back.
  3. Every finished job scores points and a host tip. Faster means more points.
  4. Let a timer run out and the guest leaves unhappy. Three misses and the evening is over.
