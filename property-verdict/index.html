The recommendation

On the numbers, Thalambur at ₹80 lakh is the stronger pick.

Casa Grande only earns its ₹40 lakh premium if it clearly beats Thalambur on land share, construction, distance to OMR or rental demand. A brand name alone does not justify it. Neither should be bought until the flood, title and rental checks further down are done.

04

Airbnb: prove it first

Neither home should be bought expecting tourist style occupancy. Semmancheri has the better short stay story, with IT staff on assignment, families visiting OMR residents and business travellers. Thalambur's short stay demand still needs proof. The Thudinest experience in Coimbatore helps with running it, but Chennai demand has to be checked on its own.

06

Before signing anything

Flood risk in Thalambur

Visit during heavy rain. Ask neighbours about past waterlogging and drainage. Never decide from a model villa.

RERA registration

Approved plans, completion commitment and the builder's delivery record.

Land title

Parent documents, Encumbrance Certificate and clear ownership, checked by an advocate.

Written Airbnb permission

From the builder or association, covering short stays, visitor access and commercial use.

Maintenance costs

Monthly charges and the sinking fund.

Handover dates

March 2027 against December 2027 is nine months. Match both to RERA, not to sales promises.

Real commute

Drive to OMR at peak hour and time it.
