Thalambur at ₹80 lakh is the stronger financial pick.

Casa Grande only earns its ₹40 lakh premium if it clearly beats Thalambur on land share, construction, distance to OMR, or rental demand. Brand name alone does not justify it. Neither property should be bought until the flood, title and rental checks below are done.

02What the extra ₹40 lakh has to earn

Casa Grande needs to rent for roughly ₹26,766 more per month just to match the EMI gap. That is about ₹3.2 lakh a year, before vacancy, maintenance, property tax and the interest on the extra loan. A brand premium rarely produces that on its own.

03Location and rental demand

  • Casa Grande (Semmancheri) sits closer to the OMR employment corridor and has more established residential demand. It should resell more easily if connectivity and amenities are good.
  • Thalambur is cheaper and can give more land or space per rupee. It reaches Navalur, Siruseri and OMR, but its value depends heavily on the exact road and neighbourhood.
  • Two to three kilometres off the main OMR road can change rental demand and resale. Treat the two localities as different markets, not interchangeable ones.
  • The Housing.com figure of about ₹9,202 per sq ft is an asking rate in Semmancheri, not a registered transaction value. Use it as a rough reference only.

04Airbnb: be cautious with both

Neither property should be bought on the assumption of tourist-style occupancy. Semmancheri has the better short stay story, with IT staff on assignment, families visiting OMR residents and business travellers. Thalambur's short stay demand needs proof. Your Thudinest experience helps with operations, but Chennai demand and occupancy have to be established independently.

Test three income models before committing:

  • Short stays (Airbnb), if the building allows it in writing.
  • Monthly furnished rentals to IT professionals.
  • Traditional 11 month residential lets as the fallback that always works.

06Before you buy either one

  • Flood risk. Visit Thalambur during heavy rain. Ask about past waterlogging and drainage. Do not decide from a model villa.
  • RERA. Confirm registration, approved plans and the completion commitment. Check the builder's delivery history.
  • Title. Get the parent documents and an Encumbrance Certificate, and confirm ownership.
  • Airbnb permission. Get written confirmation from the builder or apartment association on short stays, visitor access and commercial use.
  • Maintenance. Check monthly charges and the sinking fund.
  • Dates. March 2027 and December 2027 are nine months apart. Verify both against RERA commitments, not sales promises.
  • Commute. Measure actual travel time to OMR at peak hours.

07What would change my mind

Casa Grande becomes the better buy if it is substantially closer to OMR, has a larger land share, better construction quality and proven rental demand nearby. In that case the premium may be justified. Without those facts, I would not pay the extra ₹40 lakh for the name.
